# V0 exploratory protocol

V0 validates schemas, manipulations, prompt comprehension, tool affordances, parse
rates, variance, cost, and plausible effect directions. It may refine wording,
factor levels, power assumptions, and engineering, but cannot be reported as a
confirmatory test of the registered hypotheses.

All V0 variants retain family grouping. Any modification based on V0 creates a new
dataset/prompt version and is recorded in `CHANGELOG.md` and `DECISIONS.md`. V0 is
never pooled into V1 primary estimates.
