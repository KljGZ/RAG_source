# V1 confirmatory preregistration template

Before data collection, freeze and hash:

- hypotheses and directional predictions;
- primary and secondary endpoints;
- factor levels, contrasts, and interactions;
- model set/revisions and independent judge/human validation plan;
- family/event/root grouping and split manifest;
- inclusion, exclusion, parse, retry, and missingness policies;
- GLMM formulas, equivalence margins, cluster bootstrap, and Holm families;
- power simulations and target family counts;
- PAVG thresholds, latency/token/cost endpoints, and stopping rules;
- prompt/tool policies and all content hashes.

Any post-freeze change is versioned as an amendment before the affected outcomes are
viewed. Exploratory analyses are labeled and separated from primary results.
