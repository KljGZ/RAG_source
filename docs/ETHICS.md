# Ethics and responsible execution

The scientific value is to distinguish apparent source awareness from provenance-
grounded verification and to evaluate a defensive gate. The same manipulations could
be misused to make false material more persuasive, so generated adversarial content
is never published or redistributed as a ready-to-deploy corpus.

Real-source snapshots must record collection time, URL, digest, terms/license, and
redistribution status. Private or personal data is excluded unless separately
reviewed. Medical, legal, financial, and public-safety claims receive high-risk
labels and require domain-expert annotation before confirmatory use.

Human annotators are shown the controlled/synthetic nature of the environment,
receive uncertainty and escalation options, and are not asked to judge content
outside their competence. Disagreements are preserved rather than overwritten by a
model judge.

The project reports null results, model heterogeneity, parse/tool failures, negative
side effects of PAVG, latency/cost, and abstention trade-offs. It avoids wording that
equates probabilistic generation with an absence of understanding.
